官方插件 ​

Koishi 官方提供了许多插件。为了更好地模块化开发，它们被分散在了多个仓库中。

  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-dingtalk
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-discord
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-kook
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-lark
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-line
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-mail
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-matrix
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-qq
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-satori
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-slack
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-telegram
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-wechat-official
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-wecom
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-whatsapp
  • @koishijs/plugin-adapter-zulip

数据库支持 ​

  • @koishijs/plugin-database-memory
  • @koishijs/plugin-database-mongo
  • @koishijs/plugin-database-mysql
  • @koishijs/plugin-database-sqlite

常用功能 ​

  • @koishijs/plugin-admin：数据管理
  • @koishijs/plugin-bind：账号绑定
  • @koishijs/plugin-broadcast：发送广播
  • @koishijs/plugin-callme：设置昵称
  • @koishijs/plugin-echo：发送消息
  • @koishijs/plugin-help：查看帮助
  • @koishijs/plugin-inspect：会话信息

控制台功能 ​

  • @koishijs/plugin-analytics：数据统计
  • @koishijs/plugin-auth：用户登录
  • @koishijs/plugin-commands：指令管理
  • @koishijs/plugin-config：插件配置
  • @koishijs/plugin-console：控制台
  • @koishijs/plugin-explorer：资源管理器
  • @koishijs/plugin-insight：插件依赖图
  • @koishijs/plugin-locales：本地翻译
  • @koishijs/plugin-logger：日志管理
  • @koishijs/plugin-market：插件市场
  • @koishijs/plugin-sandbox：沙盒调试
  • @koishijs/plugin-status：运行状态

开发工具 ​

  • @koishijs/plugin-hmr：模块热替换
  • @koishijs/plugin-http：网络请求
  • @koishijs/plugin-mock：测试工具
